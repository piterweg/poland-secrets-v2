LOWER SILESIA

Castles, mountains, and spa towns.

Karkonosze, the Table Mountains, Wrocław, and dozens of castles. The region with the most diverse landscape in Poland.

Land of a Hundred Castles

Lower Silesia is Poland's most castle-rich region. Książ, Czocha, Bolczów, Chojnik — hundreds of strongholds scattered across the Sudeten hills, many preserved in excellent shape.

Add to this the spa towns — Kudowa-Zdrój, Szczawno-Zdrój, Lądek-Zdrój — and the country's oldest cities, where history interweaves with everyday life.

Wrocław and the Mountains

Wrocław, the region's capital, is a city of a hundred bridges and islands on the Oder. Perfect for a slow weekend — with coffee in Nadodrze and a morning walk through Ostrów Tumski.

The Karkonosze offer dramatic views and mountain huts where time flows slower. The Table Mountains delight with rock labyrinths, and Barycz boasts Europe's largest carp ponds.

Where to go?

Wrocław

City of a Hundred Bridges

Great coffee, islands on the Oder River, and wandering without a rigid plan.

Karkonosze

LOWER SILESIA • 2–3 days

Towards Mount Śnieżka

Waterfalls, mountain huts, and granite paths overlooking the Czech border.

Table Mountains

LOWER SILESIA • 2 days

Rock Labyrinths

Fanciful sandstone formations and scenic routes that delight hikers of all ages.

Barycz Valley

LOWER SILESIA • 2 days

Birdwatching Weekend

Milicz Ponds, cycling trails, and lively water bird mornings on Europe's largest carp ponds.

Kudowa-Zdrój

LOWER SILESIA • 2 days

Breathe in the Mountains

A historic spa resort, lush park avenues, and sweeping panoramas of the Sudeten ranges.

Mount Śnieżka

LOWER SILESIA • 1–2 days

Highest Peak of the Giant Mountains

Expansive alpine vistas, crisp high-altitude wind, and dramatic mountain weather in any season.

Samotnia Mountain Refuge

LOWER SILESIA • 1 day

Haven of Peace and Scenic Views

A legendary lakeside mountain shelter nestled under cirque cliffs, surrounded by spruce trees.

Colorful Lakes

LOWER SILESIA • 1 day

Vibrant Mineral Lakes in Rudawy

Remarkable natural colors in former pyrite quarries create a landscape that looks like a fairytale.

Szczeliniec Wielki

LOWER SILESIA • 1 day

Rock Labyrinth in the Table Mountains

Fascinating natural rock sculptures, narrow crevices, and stunning sunset views over the valley.

Książ Castle

LOWER SILESIA • 1 day

Castle Above the Precipice

The third-largest castle in Poland, hidden in the forests of Wałbrzych. Underground tunnels, garden terraces, and Sudeten panoramas.

Kłodzko

LOWER SILESIA • 1–2 days

Fortress on the Nysa Kłodzka

A town on the Czech border with an impressive fortress and Gothic bridge. Gateway to the Sudetes.

Sudeten Villages

LOWER SILESIA • 3 days

Sudeten Villages with Weaving Mills

Villages where weavers' houses have stood side by side since the 18th century. The Sudetes, where industrial history meets the mountains.
