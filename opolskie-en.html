OPOLE REGION

Fairytale castles and villages on the trail.

Moszna, Paczków, Góra Świętej Anny. A small region with the largest concentration of castles and palaces in Poland.

Fairytale Castles

The Opole Region is the most fairytale-like region in Poland. Moszna — a castle with 99 towers and an English park, one of the most beautiful palaces in Europe. Paczków — a fortified town with walls almost untouched since the 14th century.

Within 50 kilometers — a dozen castles and palaces, each with a different story. An ideal region for a weekend with children who will love the fairytale setting.

Góra Świętej Anny and Villages

Góra Świętej Anny is the oldest sanctuary in Silesia, embedded in a volcanic landscape. Amphitheater, monastery, geological trails — all surrounded by greenery.

The Opole Region is also a multicultural region — Silesian villages, German influences, Czech neighborhood. Traces of all these cultures are visible in the architecture and customs.
