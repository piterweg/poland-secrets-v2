SUBCARPATHIA

Bieszczady and wild nature.

Poland's wildest region. The Bieszczady, mountain meadows, wooden churches, and villages where time flows differently.

Land of Endless Horizons

Subcarpathia is Poland's southern-easternmost region. The Bieszczady, Połonina Wetlińska and Caryńska, Lake Solina — all places where space has real weight.

It is a land where you can drive for dozens of kilometers without meeting a living soul. Villages like Cisna, Wetlina, or Ustrzyki Górne are base camps for the wildest corners of Poland.

Wood, Icons, and Cuisine

Lemko churches scattered across the hills, icons in Sanok and Przemyśl, wooden cottages in Chyrowa — all testimonies to the region's multicultural past.

Subcarpathian cuisine offers simple, hearty flavors: pierogi with bryndza cheese, fuczki pancakes, proziaki flatbreads with garlic butter. Perfect after a day on the trail.
