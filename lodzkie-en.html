ŁÓDŹ REGION

Industrial Łódź and rivers that remember.

A region where factory history meets Poland's largest rivers — the Vistula, Warta, and Pilica.

A City in Transformation

Łódź is the most industrial city in Poland — and one of the most fascinating. Post-factory lofts, murals, the Museum of Art with the oldest modern art collection in Europe, Piotrkowska Street, Księży Młyn.

Slow travel in Łódź means walking through courtyards, coffee in OFF Piotrkowska, and slowly discovering a city that is changing before our eyes. Worth devoting a full day, not half.

Rivers and Spa

The Łódź region is a land of rivers — the Vistula, Warta, Pilica, Bzura. Kayaking, fishing, cycling along the valleys — all within an hour of Łódź.

Uniejów is Poland's only thermal spa with geothermal water. Thermal pools, a spa park, and an archbishops' castle — perfect for a weekend after sightseeing in Łódź.
