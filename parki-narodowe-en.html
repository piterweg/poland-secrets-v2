NATIONAL PARKS

23 parks that
protect Poland.

From Białowieża to Bieszczady, from the Tatras to Słowiński. A guide to the most beautiful protected corners of Poland.

What Is a National Park?

A national park is the highest form of nature protection in Poland. An area distinguished by exceptional natural, scientific, social, cultural, and educational values. Poland has 23 national parks, which together cover about 1% of the country's area.

Each park has its own character — from the Biebrza marshes to the alpine peaks of the Tatras, from the shifting dunes of Słowiński to the primeval forest of Białowieża. Slow travel through the parks is a journey through the most diverse landscapes of Poland.

From the Baltic to the Tatras

National parks stretch across all of Poland. In the north — Wolin National Park with its cliffs and bison, Słowiński National Park with shifting dunes, Tuchola Forest and Drawa National Park with pine woods. In the east — Białowieża National Park with its primeval forest, Biebrza National Park with the largest marshes in Europe, Narew National Park with the Polish Amazonia.

In the south — Tatra National Park with alpine peaks, Bieszczady National Park with mountain meadows, Pieniny National Park with the Dunajec gorge, Karkonosze National Park with Mount Śnieżka. And in the centre — Kampinos National Park near Warsaw, Holy Cross National Park with quartzite boulder fields, Wielkopolski National Park with glacial lakes.

How to Visit National Parks?

National parks have marked trails and educational paths — it is worth sticking to them. In strict reserves, entry is possible only with a guide (as in Białowieża). Many parks charge entry fees — small, supporting nature protection.

Slow travel in the parks means moving on foot or by bike — not by car. It also means being ready to wake up early (the most beautiful views are at dawn), to move in silence (animals dislike noise), and to give up some comforts (few shelters, no signal).

ALL NATIONAL PARKS

Which will you visit first?

Babia Góra National Park

LESSER POLAND • 1–2 days

Queen of the Beskids, Protected

The highest peak of the Beskids and the oldest nature reserve in Poland. Diablak, Markowe Szczawiny, and a forest that remembers the Ice Age.

Białowieża National Park

PODLASIE • 2 days

Europe's Last Primeval Forest

The oldest national park in Poland. Bison, oaks over 400 years old, and a strict reserve where nature rules by its own laws.

Biebrza National Park

PODLASIE • 2–3 days

The Largest Wetlands in Europe

A land of peat bogs, moose, and birds. The largest national park in Poland — no roads, no crowds, with wooden boardwalks over the marshes.

Tuchola Forest National Park

KUYAVIA-POMERANIA • 1–2 days

Pines, Lakes, and Silence

A park in the middle of the largest pine forest complex in Poland. Lobelia lakes, peat bogs, and trails perfect for cycling.

Drawa National Park

WEST POMERANIA • 2 days

Wild Drawa and Beavers

One of the wildest parks in Poland. The Drawa River, beavers, otters, and trails where you can walk for hours without meeting a human.

Kampinos National Park

MASOVIA • 1 day

Wilderness by Warsaw

A national park 30 km from the centre of Warsaw. Inland dunes, pine forests, and moose you can meet on the trail.

Magura National Park

LESSER POLAND • 1–2 days

Low Beskids Without Crowds

A park in the quietest corner of the Low Beskids. Lemko villages, Orthodox churches, and forests where you can walk for hours without meeting a tourist.

Narew National Park

PODLASIE • 1 day

Polish Amazonia

A park in the Narew river valley. The river flows in dozens of channels, creating a unique water labyrinth — the Polish Amazonia.

Ojców National Park

LESSER POLAND • 1 day

Limestone Cliffs and Caves

The smallest national park in Poland. Prądnik Valley, Kraków Gate, caves, and castle ruins — all in one day.

Polesie National Park

LUBLIN REGION • 1–2 days

Land of Marshes and Cranes

A park in Polesie Lubelskie. Peat bogs, cranes, European pond turtles, and trails where you only meet birds.

Słowiński National Park

POMERANIA • 1–2 days

Shifting Dunes on the Baltic

A park with shifting dunes — Polish Sahara by the sea. Łeba, Rowokół, and coastal lakes separated from the Baltic by a belt of dunes.

Tatra National Park

LESSER POLAND • 2–3 days

The Highest Mountains in Poland

The only park in Poland with an alpine character. The High Tatras, Morskie Oko, Five Lakes Valley, and trails for every level.

Ujście Warty National Park

LUBUSZ • 1 day

Bird Kingdom

The youngest national park in Poland. The Warta and Oder floodplains, 250 bird species, and observation towers.

Wielkopolski National Park

GREATER POLAND • 1 day

Lakes Near Poznań

A park 15 km from Poznań. Glacial lakes, moraine hills, and pine forests — perfect for a day trip.

Wigry National Park

PODLASIE • 1–2 days

Lake Wigry and the Monastery

A park around Lake Wigry. The Camaldolese monastery on a peninsula, glacial lakes, and trails through primeval forests.

Wolin National Park

WEST POMERANIA • 1–2 days

Cliffs and Bison on Wolin

A park on Wolin Island. Cliff coastline, bison enclosure, and the most beautiful fragments of coastal forest.
