Silesian Cuisine — Roulade, Dumplings, and Modra Kapusta

Silesian cuisine is substance, heartiness, and respect for tradition. Find out what is worth eating in Silesia — from roulade to kołocz.

Silesian cuisine is working-class cuisine — hearty, substantial, based on meat, potatoes, and cabbage. There is no finesse in it, but there is deep flavor and respect for tradition that has survived in Silesian homes to this day.

1. Silesian Roulade

Beef rolled with bacon, onion, and cucumber, stewed in sauce. It is served with Silesian dumplings and modra kapusta (braised red cabbage). A classic of Silesian cuisine that tastes like a Sunday dinner at a Silesian grandmother's.

2. Silesian Dumplings

Round potato dumplings with a characteristic indentation in the middle. They are served with meats, sauces, and goulash. The secret of good Silesian dumplings is the right proportions of boiled and raw potatoes and potato flour.

3. Modra Kapusta

Braised red cabbage with apples, onion, and vinegar. It is served with meats, especially roulade and duck. The sour-sweet flavor perfectly balances the fattiness of the meat.

4. Karminadle

Silesian meat patties — made of minced meat, bread roll, and egg, fried in a pan or stewed. They are served with potatoes, dumplings, or in sauce. A simple dish, but deeply rooted in Silesian tradition.

5. Silesian Kołocz

Sweet yeast cake with streusel and various additions — poppy seeds, cheese, fruit. It is served at weddings, holidays, and family celebrations. One of the most recognizable sweet pastries of the region.

6. Żymlok

Silesian potato sausage with the addition of bread roll, bacon, and spices. It is served hot, with mustard or onion. A traditional Silesian dish that is still served on tables in the region.

Where to Eat?

In Katowice, Gliwice, and Zabrze you will find many restaurants serving Silesian cuisine. It is also worth visiting smaller towns — there the food is more authentic and the prices lower. In search of true Silesian cuisine, it is worth asking local residents for recommended places.
