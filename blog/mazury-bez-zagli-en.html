Masuria Without Sails — 5 Ideas for Non-Sailors

Masuria is not just sails and yachts. Find out what you can do in Masuria if you don't like sailing — and still want to feel the atmosphere of the region.

Masuria is mainly associated with sailing — rightly so, as it is one of the most beautiful places in Europe for this sport. But Masuria is much more than sails. Here are 5 ideas for those who prefer calmer forms of relaxation.

1. Kayaks

Kayaks are the best way to explore Masuria without sails. The rivers — Czarna Hańcza, Krutynia, Pisa — are calm, clean, and surrounded by forests. You can paddle all day without meeting a living soul.

2. Bike

Masuria is a paradise for cyclists. The routes are well-marked, leading through villages, lakes, and forests. You can cycle the whole region — from Giżycko to Ełk — and see the most beautiful corners.

3. Masurian Villages

It is worth visiting small Masurian villages — Mikołajki, Ryn, Mrągowo. Wooden cottages, old churches, local taverns with Masurian cuisine. This is a land where time flows slower.

4. Swimming in the Lakes

Masurian lakes are clean and warm — perfect for swimming. It is worth finding a small, uncrowded beach and simply resting by the water, listening to the rustle of reeds and birdsong.

5. Boat Cruise

If you want to feel Masuria from the water but don't feel like sailing, choose a passenger boat cruise. Routes from Giżycko, Mikołajki, and Węgorzewo lead through the most beautiful lakes — Śniardwy, Mamry, Niegocin.

Where to Sleep?

Instead of popular resorts, choose agritourism in the countryside. Masurian farms offer not only accommodation but also home-cooked food, local products, and true hospitality.
