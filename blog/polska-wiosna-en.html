Poland in Spring — 7 Places That Wake Up to Life

Spring is the best time to travel around Poland. Birds return, trees bloom, and the crowds haven't arrived yet. Here are 7 places that particularly delight in spring.

Spring is the best season for traveling around Poland. Crowds have not yet arrived, prices are low, and nature wakes up to life in the most spectacular way. Here are 7 places that particularly delight in spring.

1. Biebrza — A Concert of Birds

Spring in Biebrza is a spectacle unlike anywhere else in Poland. The marshes fill with thousands of birds — cranes, geese, ducks, wading birds. This is the best time for observation — with binoculars, in silence, at dawn.

2. Roztocze — Cascades and Pine Forests

Roztocze smells of resin and damp moss in spring. The pine forests wake up to life, and the Szumy cascades on the Tanew are full of water after the thaw. It is worth taking a bike and cycling through the most beautiful villages of the region.

3. Tatras — Blooming Crocuses

Spring in the Tatras is the time of crocuses that bloom in the meadows and mountain pastures. Chochołowska Valley in April looks like a violet carpet. It is worth taking a walk and seeing this spectacle with your own eyes.

4. Bieszczady — Green Mountain Meadows

Spring in the Bieszczady is the time when mountain meadows turn lush green. Snow melts in the mountains, and fruit trees bloom in the valleys. This is the best time for hiking — without heat, without crowds.

5. Masuria — Lakes After Ice

Spring in Masuria is the time when lakes wake up after winter. Ice melts, and the water becomes crystal clear. It is worth taking a kayak — the rivers are calm, and the forests are full of birds.

6. Białowieża Forest — Green Kingdom

Spring is the best time to visit Białowieża. The forest wakes up to life, bison come out of the woods onto the meadows, and birds sing in the treetops. It is worth going with a guide — you will see more.

7. Karkonosze — Waterfalls After the Thaw

Spring in the Karkonosze is the time of waterfalls — Kamieńczyk, Szklarki, Podgórna are full of water after the thaw. It is worth taking a walk and listening to the water falling from granite walls.

How to Prepare?

A spring trip requires flexibility — the weather can be changeable. Bring a rain jacket, comfortable shoes, and good humor. Instead of planning to the minute, leave room for spontaneity — spring in Poland is unpredictable, but that is precisely why it is beautiful.
