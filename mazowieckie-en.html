MASOVIA

Warsaw and wild Kampinos.

A region where a great metropolis meets the forest. Kampinos, Zegrze Lagoon, rural Masovia, and Warsaw at a slow pace.

The Capital and the Forest

Masovia is associated with Warsaw — rightly so, as the capital dominates the region. But Masovia is also Kampinos Forest, one of the few places in Europe where wild nature borders the center of a great city.

By bike from Warsaw to Kampinos, you can arrive in an hour. When pines and dunes replace skyscrapers, it is easy to forget that you are in one of the largest metropolises in Europe.

Rural Masovia

Rural Masovia is a completely different world — flat fields, wooden churches, noble manors, and villages where time flows slower than in the capital.

It is worth turning off the Warsaw–Kampinos route and heading toward Płońsk, Ciechanów, or Pułtusk. There you can find true Masovia, with its peace and tradition.

Kurpie

MASOVIA • 2 days

Rejbak, Fafernuchy and Pierogi

Kurpie cuisine is dishes made of potatoes, flour, and mushrooms. Simple, rural, and still alive in the homes of the Kurpie people.

Kurpie

MASOVIA • 2–3 days

Kurpie Wooden Villages

White cottages with ornate shutters, paper cutouts, and wood carvings. Villages where folklore is still alive.

Łowicz Masovia

MASOVIA • 2 days

Łowicz Villages with Striped Fabrics

Colorful striped fabrics, paper cutouts, and floral motifs. Księżak villages where folklore is still a living part of everyday life.

Kampinos National Park

MASOVIA • 1 day

Wilderness by Warsaw

A national park 30 km from the centre of Warsaw. Inland dunes, pine forests, and moose you can meet on the trail.
