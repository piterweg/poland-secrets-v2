How we score

Start at 100. We subtract for rain probability, daytime temperatures below 15°C or above 28°C, wind above 20 km/h, and the weather code (rain, storm, fog). The result is the average of Saturday and Sunday, clamped to 0–100.

Where the data comes from

The forecast comes from Open-Meteo and is fetched in your browser — nothing is stored on our server. Browser cache: 30 minutes.
