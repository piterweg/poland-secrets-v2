CASTLES AND PALACES

Stone witnesses
of Polish history.

From Gothic Malbork to Baroque Książ — a guide to the most beautiful fortresses, residences, and romantic ruins. At a slow travel pace.

Castles That Tell a Story

Poland has over 400 castles and palaces, of which several dozen have survived in a form allowing a full visit. From mighty Teutonic brick fortresses, through Renaissance magnate residences, to Baroque palaces surrounded by English parks — each tells a different story.

Slow travel through Polish castles is not a marathon from gate to gate. It is rather a few hours in one place — in the courtyard, in the park, in the chapel. It is time in which you can truly feel the weight of the walls and imagine the people who lived here.

Where to Start?

If you have one weekend — Malbork. The largest brick castle in the world, inscribed on the UNESCO list, with over 200 years of construction history. If you have two days — add Książ in Lower Silesia, the third-largest castle in Poland, hidden in the forests of Wałbrzych.

For families with children — Moszna with 99 towers and a park full of old oaks. For ruin enthusiasts — Ogrodzieniec in the Kraków-Częstochowa Jura. For history lovers — Wawel and Łańcut.

How to Visit Slowly?

The rule is one: one place per day. Instead of covering three castles in one day, choose one and give it time. Visit with a guide (history becomes alive then), eat lunch in the castle café, walk through the park.

Most castles look best in the morning or at dusk — avoid the hours 11:00 AM–3:00 PM when organized groups arrive. The evening illumination of Malbork or sunset over the ruins of Ogrodzieniec are experiences you will not forget.

ALL CASTLES

Which one will you choose?

Malbork

POMERANIA • 1 day

The World's Largest Brick Castle

A Gothic fortress on the Nogat, built over 200 years. Slow exploration of cloisters, courtyards, and refectories.

Wawel

LESSER POLAND • 1 day

The Coronation Hill of Kings

Cathedral, Renaissance castle, and the dragon legend. Wawel at a slow pace means a morning without crowds and coffee with a view of the Vistula.

Książ Castle

LOWER SILESIA • 1 day

Castle Above the Precipice

The third-largest castle in Poland, hidden in the forests of Wałbrzych. Underground tunnels, garden terraces, and Sudeten panoramas.

Moszna

OPOLE REGION • 1 day

A Fairytale Castle with 99 Towers

A neo-Baroque residence surrounded by an English park. Perfect for a lazy afternoon among old oaks.

Niedzica

LESSER POLAND • 1 day

Dunajec Castle Above the Reservoir

A stronghold on a rock above Czorsztyn, with views of the Pieniny and the Tatras. Perfect for a weekend stop en route.

Ogrodzieniec

SILESIA • 1 day

Eagle's Nest on the Jura

The largest castle ruins of the Kraków-Częstochowa Jura. Limestone rocks, legends, and sunset over the valley.

Łańcut

SUBCARPATHIA • 1 day

A Magnate's Palace with Theatre

The Lubomirski and Potocki residence with an English park. Slow tour of the interiors, orchid house, and carriage house.

Pszczyna

SILESIA • 1 day

Renaissance Pearl of Upper Silesia

A palace with preserved 19th-century interiors and an English-style park. A town with a postcard market square.
