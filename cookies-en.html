Cookie Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on the User's device (computer, tablet, smartphone) while using the Website. They contain information used for various purposes — from ensuring the proper functioning of the Website, through traffic analysis, to personalization of content.

2. What Cookies Do We Use?

On the polandsecrets.com website, we use the following categories of cookies:

a) Essential (technical) cookies

They ensure the proper functioning of the Website. Without them, the site does not work correctly.

  • pb-saved — remembers the list of destinations saved by the User (localStorage).
  • cookie-consent — remembers the User's choice regarding cookie consents.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest — necessary to provide the service).

b) Analytical cookies

They help understand how Users use the Website. They collect data in aggregated and anonymous form.

  • _ga, _ga_G-2P2RTBRDJ5 — Google Analytics 4, user and session identification.
  • _gid — Google Analytics, user identification (short-term).

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR (User's consent).

c) Marketing cookies

Used to display ads tailored to the User's interests (if the Website uses such tools).

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR (User's consent).

3. Managing Consents

On the first visit to the Website, a banner is displayed that allows managing cookie consents. The User can:

  • Accept all — consent to all categories of cookies.
  • Reject optional — accept only essential cookies.
  • Customize — choose specific cookie categories.

The choice can be changed at any time by clicking the Cookie settings link in the footer or by clearing your browser data.

4. How to Disable Cookies in Your Browser?

The User can manage cookies in their browser settings. Instructions for the most popular browsers:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

Disabling cookies may affect the functionality of the Website — some features may stop working correctly.

More information: Google Privacy Policy.
