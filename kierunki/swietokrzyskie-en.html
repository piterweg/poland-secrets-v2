HOLY CROSS REGION

Holy Cross Mountains — Gentle Hills, Deep Stillness

1–2 days
Home›Holy Cross Region›Holy Cross Mountains

The Świętokrzyskie Mountains are the oldest mountains in Poland. Bare rocks, monastery on Łysa Góra, caves. Slow travel through the Świętokrzyskie Mountains.

Why it's worth it

The Świętokrzyskie Mountains are the oldest mountains in Poland — their age is estimated at about 500 million years. They lie in the Świętokrzyskie voivodeship, in central-eastern Poland. The highest peak is Łysica (614 m), but the symbol of the region is Łysa Góra (595 m) with a monastery on the summit. The Świętokrzyskie Mountains are low but very diverse: bare rocks, boulder fields, fir forests, scree, and caves. Add to that cultural heritage: the monastery on Łysa Góra, which was a pilgrimage site as early as the Middle Ages, and the sanctuary at Święty Krzyż.

Slow travel through the Świętokrzyskie Mountains is a journey through a landscape that surprises with its diversity. The region has mountains, monasteries, caves, castles, and villages — all within a few dozen kilometres. It is worth spending 2–3 days here to truly feel this landscape. The best way to explore the region is on foot or by car — there are about 300 km of hiking trails, including the famous Edmund Massalski Trail. Public transport from Kielce is good (buses, trains).

What to see

The Świętokrzyskie Mountains are divided into three main parts: the Łysogóry Range (centre), the Jeleniów Range (east), and the Chęciny Range (west). Exploring the mountains takes 2–3 days.

Łysogóry Range

  • Łysa Góra — a peak with a monastery, one of the most important sanctuaries in Poland. Worth it for architecture.
  • Boulder fields — rock rubble on the slopes of Łysogóry, unique in Poland. Worth it for views.
  • Święty Krzyż — a sanctuary on Łysa Góra, with relics of the Holy Cross. Worth it for history.

Jeleniów Range

  • Jeleniowska Góra — a peak in the Jeleniów Range (536 m). Worth it for views.
  • Krzyżtopór Castle — ruins of a castle in Ujazd, one of the largest in Poland. Worth it for architecture.
  • Ujazd village — a village with Krzyżtopór Castle. Worth it for architecture.

Chęciny Range

  • Chęciny Castle — ruins of a castle on a rock, with a viewpoint. Worth it for architecture.
  • Raj Cave — a karst cave, one of the most beautiful in Poland. Worth it for nature.
  • Chęciny — a small town at the foot of the castle, with a market square and church. Worth it for architecture.

When to go

  • May and June — the best time for slow travel. Warm, green, few tourists.
  • July–August — trail season. Worth it for hiking, but crowded on Łysa Góra.
  • September–October — second best. Autumn colours, fewer people, mushroom picking in the forests.
  • November–March — the region is sleepy in winter, many attractions closed. Worth it for those seeking silence.

Getting there

  • By car: ~2 hours from Warsaw (S7), ~2 hours from Kraków (DK73), ~2 hours from Łódź (S7).
  • By train: Kielce, Chęciny, Ostrowiec Świętokrzyski have railway stations.
  • By bus: PKS serves most towns and villages.

The Świętokrzyskie Mountains are best explored by car or on foot — the region is vast, and public transport between villages can be sparse. Leave the car at the car park in Nowa Słupia or Chęciny.

How much time you need

A weekend (2 days) is the minimum — Łysa Góra, Chęciny Castle.

Three days is realistic for a first visit. Łysa Góra, Chęciny Castle, Raj Cave, Krzyżtopór.

A week lets you explore the whole region — from Kielce to Ostrowiec, with numerous stops.

What to eat and where to sleep

  • Pierogi with cabbage and mushrooms — a Świętokrzyskie classic.
  • Świętokrzyskie kwaśnica — sour cabbage soup with white sausage.
  • Goat cheeses — from local farms, available in village shops.
  • Beer from Świętokrzyskie breweries — several craft breweries in the region.

Accommodation: Kielce (city, more options), Nowa Słupia (village, guesthouses), Chęciny (small town, guesthouses), Święty Krzyż (monastery, guesthouses).

Practical tips

  • Guided tours — the region has rich history, take a guide at Święty Krzyż.
  • Raj Cave — daily entry limits apply. Book tickets in advance.
  • Shopping — buy honey, cheese, and cold cuts directly from farmers.
  • Edmund Massalski Trail — the main trail of the Świętokrzyskie Mountains, about 80 km. Worth hiking in 3–4 days.
  • Weather — weather can be changeable, even in July bring a jacket.
  • Signal — good throughout the region, including in the mountains.

Trip plan

  1. Viewpoint atop Mount Łysica
  2. Walk along the ancient forested ridges
  3. Coffee and regional treats after the hike

Tip

Climb Łysa Góra early in the morning — the best time to see the monastery without crowds and with good light for photos.
