GREATER POLAND

Wielkopolski National Park — Lakes Near Poznań

1 day
Home›Greater Poland›Wielkopolski National Park

Wielkopolska National Park is a land of moraines, lakes, and forests. Near Poznań, one of the oldest parks in Poland. Slow travel through Greater Poland.

Why it's worth it

Wielkopolska National Park is one of the oldest national parks in Poland (established 1957). It lies 15 km from Poznań, in the middle of Greater Poland. It protects a unique post-glacial landscape: moraines, ribbon lakes, hills, inland dunes. This landscape was formed during the last glaciation — hence the name “moraines”. Add to that forests: pine forests, oak-hornbeam forests, alder carrs, and numerous lakes: Góreckie, Kociołek, Skrzynka, Witobelskie.

Slow travel through Wielkopolska National Park is a journey through a landscape that surprises with its diversity. The park has several lakes, moraine hills, and forests — all within a few kilometres of each other. It is worth spending 1–2 days here to truly feel this landscape. The best way to explore the park is on foot or by bike — there are about 80 km of hiking and cycling trails. Public transport from Poznań is excellent (buses, trains to Puszczykowo and Mosina).

What to see

Wielkopolska National Park is divided into three main parts: ribbon lakes (centre), moraine hills (east), and forests (west). Exploring the park takes 1–2 days.

Ribbon lakes

  • Lake Góreckie — the largest lake in the park, with an island and a castle on a hill. Worth it for views.
  • Lake Kociołek — a lake in the middle of the forest, with crystal-clear water. Worth it for swimming.
  • Lake Skrzynka — a lake with an island, in the northern part of the park. Worth it for nature.

Moraine hills

  • Castle Hill — a hill above Lake Góreckie, with castle ruins. Worth it for views.
  • Osowa Góra Hill — the highest hill in the park (132 m). Worth it for views.
  • “Moraines” trail — a nature trail through the moraine hills. Worth it for nature.

Forests

  • Pine forests — extensive forests in the western part of the park. Worth it for mushroom picking.
  • Oak-hornbeam forests — deciduous forests with oaks and hornbeams. Worth it for nature.
  • “Pine forests” trail — a nature trail through the pine forests. Worth it for nature.

When to go

  • April–May — the best time for nature. Blooming meadows, birds at peak nesting.
  • May and June — the best time for slow travel. Warm, green, few tourists.
  • July–August — warm, but crowded (the park is popular with Poznań residents).
  • September–October — second best. Autumn colours, fewer people, mushroom picking.
  • November–March — the park is accessible in winter, but many attractions closed. Worth it for those seeking silence.

Getting there

  • By car: ~20 minutes from Poznań (DK5), ~2 hours from Wrocław (S5), ~3 hours from Warsaw (A2).
  • By train: Puszczykowo, Mosina have railway stations, with connections from Poznań.
  • By bus: MPK and PKS lines from Poznań to Puszczykowo and Mosina.

Wielkopolska National Park is best explored on foot or by bike — the park is well marked, and connections with Poznań are excellent. Leave the car at the car park in Puszczykowo or Mosina.

How much time you need

Half a day is the minimum — Lake Góreckie and Castle Hill.

A weekend (2 days) is realistic for a first visit. Lake Góreckie, Castle Hill, Lake Kociołek, moraine hills.

Three days lets you explore the whole park and surroundings — from Puszczykowo to Mosina.

What to eat and where to sleep

  • Pierogi with cabbage and mushrooms — a Greater Poland classic, available at inns.
  • Pyry z gzikiem — potatoes with curd cheese, a Greater Poland classic.
  • Duck with apples — from local farms, available at inns.
  • Beer from Greater Poland breweries — several craft breweries in the region.

Accommodation: Poznań (city, more options), Puszczykowo (small town, guesthouses), Mosina (small town, guesthouses), villages (agritourism).

Practical tips

  • Guided tours — the park has rich nature and history, take a guide.
  • Lakes — swimming is forbidden in some lakes in the park. Check before entering the water.
  • Shopping — buy honey, cheese, and cold cuts directly from farmers.
  • Opening hours — some attractions (museum, viewing tower) open only in season.
  • Weather — weather can be changeable, even in July bring a jacket.
  • Signal — good throughout the park (close to Poznań).

Trip plan

  1. Lake Góreckie
  2. Osowa Góra hill
  3. Trail in Puszczykowo

Tip

Climb Castle Hill above Lake Góreckie early in the morning — the view over the lake and castle ruins in morning light is a Greater Poland classic.
