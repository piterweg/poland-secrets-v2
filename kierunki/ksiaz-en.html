LOWER SILESIA

Książ Castle — Castle Above the Precipice

1 day
Home›Lower Silesia›Książ Castle

The third-largest castle in Poland, hidden in the forests of Wałbrzych. Underground tunnels, garden terraces, and Sudeten panoramas. Slow travel through Książ Castle.

Why Książ Castle, differently

Książ Castle is one of the largest and most beautiful residences in Poland, hidden among the forests of Wałbrzych. Surrounded by ravines and rocky cliffs, it towers over the Pełcznica valley and offers views that take your breath away. It is the third-largest castle in Poland (after Malbork and Wawel), with over 400 rooms and 11 towers.

Slow travel through Książ Castle is a journey through layers of history. The history of Książ is a story of power and tragedy — from the Hochberg family, through World War II, to its post-war fate. A guided tour of the castle allows you to understand not only the architecture but also the dramatic fates of its residents. Garden terraces, underground tunnels, the ballroom — every element of this place tells a story you won't find in any textbook. This is a land that rewards those who slow down.

What to see

Książ Castle is not just the castle itself — it is a whole complex of attractions: garden terraces, underground routes, the Palm House, stables. One day is enough to see it, but it is worth devoting a full day.

Castle and chambers

  • Książ Castle — a Baroque-Renaissance castle from the 13th century, rebuilt in the 18th and 20th centuries. A guided tour takes ~2 hours. Worth it for architecture and history.
  • Maximilian Hall — the largest hall of the castle (15 m high), with a richly decorated vault. Worth it for architecture.
  • Ballroom — a ceremonial hall from the 19th century, where balls and receptions were held. Worth it for interiors.
  • Library and Study — private chambers of the Hochbergs, with original furnishings. Worth it for understanding the daily life of the aristocracy.
  • Garden terraces — terraces from the 19th century, stretching over several levels. In spring and summer full of flowers, in autumn — golden and russet. Worth it for views and a walk.

Underground and Palm House

  • Underground tourist route — a route through tunnels carved in rock during World War II (~2 km). Worth it for history and atmosphere.
  • Palm House — the former Hochberg Palm House, today restored, with a collection of exotic plants. Worth it for contrast with the castle.
  • Stables — the former Hochberg stables, today an equestrian centre. Worth it for horse lovers.
  • Castle chapel — a Baroque chapel from the 18th century, with rich decoration. Worth it for architecture.

Surroundings

  • Wałbrzych — a town at the foot of the castle, with a market square and accommodation base. Worth a walk and shopping.
  • Grodno Castle — ruins of a castle in Zagórze Śląskie, ~15 km from Książ. Worth it for architecture and views.
  • Bolczów Castle — ruins of a castle in Rudawy Janowickie, ~25 km from Książ. Worth it for history.
  • Świdnica — a town with the Church of Peace (UNESCO), ~25 km from Książ. Worth it for architecture.
  • Jawor — a town with the Church of Peace (UNESCO), ~40 km from Książ. Worth it for architecture.

When to go

  • May and June — the best time. Terraces bloom, fewer tourists, long days. Worth a walk on the terraces.
  • September and October — second best. Autumn colours in the park, clear air, fewer people.
  • July and August — peak season. Castle crowded, ticket queues long, car parks full. If you must — pick a weekday and come in the morning.
  • November–March — the castle is less crowded in winter, but terraces are closed. Worth it for those who want to see the castle without crowds.

Getting there

  • By car: ~1.5 hours from Wrocław (A4 and DK3), ~3 hours from Prague, ~4 hours from Berlin, ~5 hours from Warsaw. Car park at the castle (paid, in season full from 9 a.m.).
  • By train: station in Wałbrzych, with connections from Wrocław (~2 hours), Prague (~4 hours). Then bus or taxi to the castle (~15 minutes).
  • By bus: PKS and private operators from Wrocław and Wałbrzych.

Within Wałbrzych you can walk or use local minibuses. To the castle leads a road from Wałbrzych (~15 minutes by car).

How much time you need

A full day (5–6 hours) is the minimum for a leisurely visit to the castle and terraces.

A weekend (2 days) lets you add the underground and Palm House, as well as the surroundings (Grodno Castle, Świdnica).

A week lets you explore the whole region — Książ, Wałbrzych, Grodno Castle, Świdnica, Jawor, Karkonosze.

What to eat and where to sleep

  • Lower Silesian cuisine — duck with apples, Silesian dumplings, kwaśnica. Worth trying in local inns.
  • Grilled trout — from local farms in the Sudetes.
  • Grilled oscypek — smoked sheep cheese with cranberry.
  • Beer from local breweries — several craft breweries in the Sudetes (Browar Wielka Sowa, Browar Złoty Pies).
  • Coffee and cake — Wałbrzych and Świdnica have several good cafés.
  • Korycin cheeses — smoked and aged, available in local shops.

Accommodation: Wałbrzych (largest choice, but a town), Szczawno-Zdrój (spa, quiet), Świdnica (town with the Church of Peace, more options), Książ (hotel in the castle, expensive but atmospheric), Jelenia Góra (~50 km, more options).

Practical tips

  • Bookings — tickets for the castle and the underground route are worth booking in advance, especially in season. The underground route has a limited number of places.
  • Guided tours — the history of Książ is complex, and without context many rooms look similar. Worth going with a guide.
  • Garden terraces — walking the terraces is possible only in season (April–October). Plan your visit in that period.
  • Underground — the underground route is available all year, but requires comfortable shoes (uneven surfaces, stairs).
  • Crowds — the castle is crowded in season (May–September). Come in the morning.
  • Parking — the car park at the castle is paid. In season it fills from 9 a.m.
  • Signal — good throughout the castle complex and in Wałbrzych.

Trip plan

  1. Chambers and ballroom tour
  2. Walk through the garden terraces
  3. Underground tourist route

Tip

Visit Książ with a guide. The history of this place is so complex that without proper context, the castle's architecture will remain just a backdrop.
