WEST POMERANIA

Wolin National Park — Cliffs and Bison on Wolin

1–2 days
Home›West Pomerania›Wolin National Park

Wolin National Park is a land of cliffs, bison, and Baltic coastline. Gosań Cliff, bison enclosure. Slow travel on Wolin Island.

Why it's worth it

Wolin National Park is one of two coastal parks in Poland (alongside Słowiński). It lies on Wolin Island, in the West Pomeranian voivodeship. It protects a unique landscape: high cliffs (up to 95 m — the highest in Poland), sandy beaches, Baltic pine forests, and lakes. The park's greatest attraction are the cliffs — active and dead, made of clay and sand, which slide into the sea. Add to that bison — a free population of these animals lives in the park, one of the few in Poland.

Slow travel through Wolin National Park is a journey through a landscape that surprises with its diversity. The park has cliffs, beaches, pine forests, lakes, and bison — all within a few kilometres of each other. It is worth spending 2–3 days here to truly feel this landscape. The best way to explore the park is on foot or by bike — there are about 50 km of hiking and cycling trails. Public transport from Międzyzdroje is good (buses, ferries).

What to see

Wolin National Park is divided into three main parts: cliffs (west), pine forests (centre), and lakes (east). Exploring the park takes 2–3 days.

Cliffs

  • Gosań Cliff — the highest cliff in Poland (95 m), with a viewpoint. Worth it for views.
  • Kawcza Góra Cliff — a cliff above Międzyzdroje, with a viewpoint. Worth it for views.
  • “Cliffs” trail — a nature trail along the cliffs. Worth it for views.

Pine forests

  • Baltic pine forests — extensive forests in the centre of the park. Worth it for mushroom picking.
  • Bison enclosure — an enclosure where you can see bison up close. Worth it for nature.
  • “Pine forests” trail — a nature trail through the forests. Worth it for nature.

Lakes

  • Lake Koprowo — a lake in the middle of the park, with crystal-clear water. Worth it for swimming.
  • Turquoise Lake — a karst lake with turquoise water, the only one of its kind in Poland. Worth it for views.
  • “Lakes” trail — a nature trail around the lakes. Worth it for nature.

When to go

  • May and June — the best time for slow travel. Warm, green, few tourists.
  • July–August — beach and swimming season. Worth it for the sea, but crowded in Międzyzdroje.
  • September–October — second best. Autumn colours, fewer people, mushroom picking in the forests.
  • November–March — the park is accessible in winter, but cold and windy. Worth it for those seeking silence.

Getting there

  • By car: ~1.5 hours from Szczecin (DK3), ~3 hours from Berlin (A11), ~4 hours from Poznań (S3).
  • By train: Międzyzdroje, Świnoujście have railway stations. Then rent a bike.
  • By bus: PKS serves Międzyzdroje, Świnoujście, Wisełka.

Wolin National Park is best explored on foot or by bike — the park is well marked, and trails are well maintained. Leave the car at the car park in Międzyzdroje.

How much time you need

A weekend (2 days) is the minimum — Gosań Cliff, bison enclosure, Turquoise Lake.

Three days is realistic for a first visit. Gosań Cliff, bison enclosure, Turquoise Lake, Lake Koprowo, Baltic pine forests.

A week lets you explore the whole park and surroundings — from Międzyzdroje to Świnoujście.

What to eat and where to sleep

  • Smoked fish — from smokehouses in Międzyzdroje and Świnoujście.
  • Baltic herring — a coastal classic, available at fish shops.
  • Fish soup — from local fish, available at inns.
  • Beer from West Pomeranian breweries — several craft breweries in the region.

Accommodation: Międzyzdroje (small town, more options), Świnoujście (town, more options), Wisełka (village, guesthouses), numerous agritourism farms in villages.

Practical tips

  • Guided tours — the park has rich nature, take a guide.
  • Cliffs — walking on the cliffs outside marked trails is forbidden. Stick to the paths.
  • Bison — a free population of bison lives in the park. Keep your distance and do not approach too closely.
  • Shopping — buy honey, cheese, and cold cuts directly from farmers.
  • Weather — coastal weather can be changeable, even in July bring a jacket and a hat.
  • Signal — good in towns, weaker in the park. Have an offline map.

Trip plan

  1. Gosań cliff
  2. Bison enclosure in Karsibór
  3. View from Kawcza Góra

Tip

Go to Gosań Cliff early in the morning — the best time to see the cliff without crowds and with good light for photos.
