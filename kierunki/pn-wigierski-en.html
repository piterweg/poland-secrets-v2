Wigry National Park — Lake Wigry and the Monastery

1–2 days
Home›Podlasie›Wigry National Park

Wigry National Park is a land of lakes, forests, and a monastery. Lake Wigry, Augustów Forest. Slow travel through Suwałki region.

Why it's worth it

Wigry National Park is one of the largest national parks in Poland (~150 km²). It lies in the Suwałki region, in northeastern Poland, on the border with Lithuania. It protects a unique post-glacial landscape: Lake Wigry (one of the deepest in Poland), numerous smaller lakes, the forests of the Augustów Forest, and the Camaldolese monastery on a peninsula on Lake Wigry. This is a park where water plays the main role — lakes make up about 1/3 of the park's area.

Slow travel through Wigry National Park is a journey through a landscape that surprises with its diversity. The park has lakes, forest, a monastery, and fishing villages — all within a few kilometres of each other. It is worth spending 2–3 days here to truly feel this landscape. The best way to explore the park is on foot, by bike, or by kayak — there are about 200 km of hiking and cycling trails and kayak routes. Public transport from Suwałki is good (buses, trains).

What to see

Wigry National Park is divided into three main parts: Lake Wigry (centre), the Augustów Forest (east), and fishing villages (west). Exploring the park takes 2–3 days.

Lake Wigry

  • Lake Wigry — the largest lake in the park (21.8 km²), with numerous bays and islands. Worth it for kayaking.
  • Camaldolese monastery — a Baroque monastery on a peninsula, one of the most valuable monuments in the region. Worth it for architecture.
  • “Around Wigry” trail — a hiking trail around the lake, about 30 km. Worth it for views.

Augustów Forest

  • Augustów Forest — an extensive forest complex, one of the largest in Poland. Worth it for mushroom picking.
  • “Forest” trail — a nature trail through the forest. Worth it for nature.
  • Wysoki Most village — a village in the middle of the forest, with a wooden church. Worth it for the atmosphere.

Fishing villages

  • Stary Folwark village — a village with the park management and a museum. Worth it for information.
  • Wigry village — a village with the monastery and a harbour. Worth it for architecture.
  • Bryzgiel village — a fishing village on the lake, with a fishing tradition. Worth it for culture.

When to go

  • May and June — the best time for slow travel. Warm, green, few tourists.
  • July–August — kayaking and swimming season. Worth it for lakes, but crowded in Stary Folwark.
  • September–October — second best. Autumn colours, fewer people, mushroom picking in the forest.
  • November–March — the park is accessible in winter, but cold. Worth it for those seeking silence.

Getting there

  • By car: ~15 minutes from Suwałki (DK8), ~1.5 hours from Białystok (DK8), ~4 hours from Warsaw (S8).
  • By train: Suwałki, Augustów have railway stations. Then rent a car or bike.
  • By bus: PKS serves Suwałki, Stary Folwark, Wigry.

Wigry National Park is best explored on foot, by bike, or by kayak — the park is well marked, and trails are well maintained. Leave the car at the car park in Stary Folwark.

How much time you need

A weekend (2 days) is the minimum — Lake Wigry, Camaldolese monastery, Stary Folwark.

Three days is realistic for a first visit. Lake Wigry, Camaldolese monastery, Stary Folwark, Augustów Forest, Bryzgiel.

A week lets you explore the whole park and surroundings — from Suwałki to Augustów, with numerous stops.

What to eat and where to sleep

  • Sielawa — dried fish, a Suwałki classic.
  • Kartacze — potato dumplings with meat, a Suwałki classic.
  • Trout from Wigry — from local farms, available at inns.
  • Goat cheeses — from local farms, available in village shops.

Accommodation: Suwałki (city, more options), Stary Folwark (village, guesthouses), Wigry (village, agritourism), Augustów (town, more options).

Practical tips

  • Guided tours — the park has rich nature and history, take a guide.
  • Kayaks — Lake Wigry is a great place for kayaking. Rentals in Stary Folwark and Wigry.
  • Shopping — buy honey, cheese, and cold cuts directly from farmers.
  • Opening hours — the Camaldolese monastery is open only on selected days. Check before your trip.
  • Weather — Suwałki region weather can be changeable, even in July bring a jacket.
  • Signal — good in towns, weaker in the forest. Have an offline map.

Trip plan

  1. Camaldolese monastery in Wigry
  2. Cruise on Lake Wigry
  3. Trail around the lake

Tip

Rent a kayak in Stary Folwark and paddle around the peninsula with the Camaldolese monastery — the best way to see Wigry from the water.
