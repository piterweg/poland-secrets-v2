Narew National Park — Polish Amazonia

1 day
Home›Podlasie›Narew National Park

Narew National Park is a paradise for birds. Marshes, Narew floodplains, swans, and cranes. Slow travel through Podlasie.

Why it's worth it

Narew National Park was established in 1996 to protect the marshy valley of the upper Narew — one of the most valuable wetland areas in Poland. The park is small (~73 km²), but exceptionally rich in nature. The Narew valley is a mosaic of floodplains, marshes, meadows, and reed beds — in spring, when the Narew floods, the whole area turns into one great lake. This is a paradise for birds: about 200 species live in the park, including many rare and endangered ones. Add to that beavers, otters, moose.

Slow travel through Narew National Park is a journey through a landscape that requires humility. The park has no peaks, no waterfalls — there are marshes, floodplains, and birds. It is worth spending 1–2 days here to truly feel this landscape. The best way to explore the park is on foot or by kayak — there are several nature trails and a kayak route on the Narew. Public transport is weak, so rent a car.

What to see

Narew National Park is divided into three main parts: the Narew valley (centre), marshes in the northern part, and meadows in the southern part. Exploring the park takes 1–2 days.

Narew valley

  • Narew — the river, its valley is the most valuable element of the park. Worth it for a kayak trip.
  • Kurowo village — a village by the park, seat of the park management. Worth it for information.
  • Waniewo village — a village with a wooden church and a viewpoint. Worth it for views.

Northern marshes

  • Waniewo marsh — the most valuable ecosystem in the park, a refuge for wetland birds. Worth it for nature.
  • “Kładka” trail — a nature trail over the marshes, with a viewing tower. Worth it for views.
  • “Kurowo” trail — a nature trail around the Narew floodplains. Worth it for nature.

Southern meadows

  • Narew meadows — extensive meadows, flooded in spring. Worth it for birds.
  • Rzędziany village — a village with a wooden church. Worth it for architecture.
  • Pańki village — a village with a viewpoint over the Narew valley. Worth it for views.

When to go

  • April–May — the best time for birds. Spring floods, birds at peak nesting. Worth it for nature.
  • May and June — the best time for slow travel. Warm, green, few tourists.
  • July–August — warm, but fewer birds (post-breeding period).
  • September–October — second best. Autumn bird migrations.
  • November–March — the park is accessible in winter, but less interesting for nature. Worth it for those seeking silence.

Getting there

  • By car: ~30 minutes from Białystok (DK8), ~2.5 hours from Warsaw (S8), ~3 hours from Lublin (S19).
  • By train: Białystok, Łapy have railway stations. Then rent a car.
  • By bus: PKS serves most larger villages, but connections are sparse.

Narew National Park is best explored by car or on foot — the park is small, but public transport is weak.

How much time you need

Half a day is the minimum — the “Kładka” trail and a viewpoint.

A weekend (2 days) is realistic for a first visit. “Kładka” trail, “Kurowo” trail, a Narew kayak trip.

Three days lets you explore the whole park and surroundings — from Waniewo to Rzędziany.

What to eat and where to sleep

  • Pierogi with potatoes — a Podlasie classic, available at inns.
  • Potato kiszka — a Podlasie speciality, available at inns.
  • Trout from the Narew — from local farms, available at inns.
  • Goat cheeses — from local farms, available in village shops.

Accommodation: Białystok (city, more options), Łapy (small town, guesthouses), Kurowo (village, agritourism), numerous agritourism farms in villages.

Practical tips

  • Guided tours — the park has rich birdlife, take a guide.
  • Binoculars — essential equipment. Without binoculars you miss half the experience.
  • Shopping — buy honey, cheese, and cold cuts directly from farmers.
  • Opening hours — trails are open year-round, but sections may be closed during the breeding season.
  • Weather — Podlasie weather can be changeable, even in July bring a jacket.
  • Signal — weak in many parts of the park. Have an offline map.

Trip plan

  1. Śliwno-Waniewo boardwalk
  2. Observation tower in Waniewo
  3. Kayaking on the Narew

Tip

Go to the “Kładka” trail in Kurowo at dawn — the best time to hear and see marsh birds.
