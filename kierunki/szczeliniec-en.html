Szczeliniec Wielki — Rock Labyrinth in the Table Mountains

1 day
Home›Lower Silesia›Szczeliniec Wielki

Szczeliniec Wielki is the highest peak of the Table Mountains. Rock labyrinth, viewing terraces, mountain hut. Slow travel through the Sudetes.

Why it's worth it

Szczeliniec Wielki is the highest peak of the Table Mountains (919 m) and one of the most interesting peaks in Poland. It lies in the Lower Silesian voivodeship, in the Sudetes, within the Table Mountains National Park. Szczeliniec is a peak that has everything an interesting peak should have: a rock labyrinth, viewing terraces, a mountain hut, and a unique landscape. The rock labyrinth on the summit is a true one-of-a-kind — a system of crevices, corridors, and rock towers where you can get lost. Add to that viewing terraces from which, on a clear day, you can see the whole Sudetes and even the Tatras.

Slow travel through Szczeliniec is a journey through a landscape that surprises with its diversity. The peak has a labyrinth, terraces, a mountain hut, and trails — all within a few hundred metres. It is worth spending 1–2 days here to truly feel this landscape. The best way to explore Szczeliniec is on foot — two trails lead to the summit (from Karłów and from Polanica), both well marked. Public transport from Kłodzko and Kudowa is good (buses).

What to see

Szczeliniec Wielki is divided into three main parts: the rock labyrinth (summit), viewing terraces (around the summit), and the mountain hut (on the summit). Exploring the peak takes 1–2 days.

Rock labyrinth

  • Rock labyrinth — a system of crevices, corridors, and rock towers. Worth it for views.
  • “Piekiełko” corridor — the narrowest corridor in the labyrinth, about 60 cm wide. Worth it for adrenaline.
  • Rock towers — numerous rock towers with distinctive shapes. Worth it for views.

Viewing terraces

  • Main Viewing Terrace — the main terrace on the summit, with a panorama of the Sudetes. Worth it for views.
  • “Photographer” Terrace — a terrace with a view of Śnieżka and the Karkonosze. Worth it for views.
  • “Eagle” Terrace — a terrace with a view of the Bystrzyckie Mountains. Worth it for views.

Mountain hut

  • PTTK Mountain Hut — a hut on the summit, one of the oldest in the Sudetes. Worth it for a rest.
  • Buffet — a buffet in the hut, with warm meals. Worth it for a rest.
  • Viewpoint at the hut — a terrace at the hut, with a panorama. Worth it for views.

When to go

  • May and June — the best time for slow travel. Warm, green, few tourists.
  • July–August — trail season. Worth it for hiking, but crowded on the summit.
  • September–October — second best. Autumn colours, fewer people.
  • November–March — the summit is accessible in winter, but requires experience. Worth it for those seeking silence.

Getting there

  • By car: ~1.5 hours from Wrocław (DK8), ~2 hours from Prague (D11), ~4 hours from Berlin (A4 + DK8).
  • By train: Kudowa-Zdrój, Kłodzko have railway stations. Then rent a car or take a minibus.
  • By bus: PKS serves Karłów, Kudowa, Polanica.

Szczeliniec is best explored on foot — two trails lead to the summit (from Karłów and from Polanica), both well marked. Leave the car at the car park in Karłów or Polanica.

How much time you need

Half a day is the minimum — summit climb and labyrinth.

A weekend (2 days) is realistic for a first visit. Szczeliniec, labyrinth, Błędne Skały, Karłów.

Three days lets you explore the whole Table Mountains National Park — from Szczeliniec to Błędne Skały.

What to eat and where to sleep

  • Pierogi with meat — a Lower Silesian classic, available at inns.
  • Duck with apples — from local farms, available at inns.
  • Trout from the Bystrzyca — from local farms, available at inns.
  • Beer from Lower Silesian breweries — several craft breweries in the region.

Accommodation: Kudowa-Zdrój (town, more options), Polanica-Zdrój (spa, guesthouses), Karłów (village, guesthouses), Duszniki-Zdrój (spa, guesthouses).

Practical tips

  • Guided tours — Szczeliniec has rich history and nature, take a guide.
  • Rock labyrinth — you can get lost in the labyrinth. Stick to the marked trail.
  • Shopping — buy honey, cheese, and cold cuts directly from farmers.
  • Tickets — park entry tickets are mandatory. Buy online in advance.
  • Weather — Sudeten weather can be changeable, even in July bring a jacket.
  • Signal — good throughout the region, weaker in higher mountain sections.

Trip plan

  1. Walk through the sandstone labyrinth
  2. Nearby Błędne Skały rock maze
  3. Relaxing break in the mountain haven

Tip

Climb Szczeliniec early in the morning — the best time to see the labyrinth without crowds and with good light for photos.
