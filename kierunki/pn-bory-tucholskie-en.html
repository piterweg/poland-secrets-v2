Tuchola Forest National Park — Pines, Lakes, and Silence

1–2 days
Home›Kuyavia-Pomerania›Tuchola Forest National Park

A park in the middle of the largest pine forest complex in Poland. Lobelia lakes, peat bogs, and trails perfect for cycling.

A Park in the Middle of Pine Forests

Tuchola Forest National Park is a relatively young park — established in 1996 — but it protects one of the most valuable natural areas in Poland. It is located in the middle of the largest pine forest complex in Poland — the Tuchola Forest, which stretches over 3,000 km².

The park is famous for its lobelia lakes — unique water bodies with very clean water, where rare plants grow, including the water lobelia. This is one of the most valuable types of lakes in Europe, of which there are only a dozen or so in Poland.

By Bike Through the Park

Tuchola Forest National Park is the perfect place for cycling trips. Several cycling routes pass through the park and its surroundings, leading through the most beautiful corners of the Tuchola Forest. You can cycle dozens of kilometers here without meeting a living soul.

It is also worth stopping by Lake Charzykowskie — one of the largest lakes in the region, a paradise for sailors and kayakers. In summer, regattas take place here, and in winter — iceboat races on the ice.

Trip plan

  1. Nature trail by Lake Charzykowskie
  2. Kulawa River Valley Reserve
  3. Cycling route around the park

Tip

Rent a bike in Charzykowy or Drzycim — it is the best way to see the park and the surrounding lakes. By car you will see much less.
