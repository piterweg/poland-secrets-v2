LOWER SILESIA

Colorful Lakes — Vibrant Mineral Lakes in Rudawy

1 day
Home›Lower Silesia›Colorful Lakes

Remarkable natural colors in former pyrite quarries create a landscape that looks like a fairytale. Slow travel through the Colorful Lakes in Rudawy Janowickie.

Why the Colorful Lakes, differently

The Colorful Lakes in Rudawy Janowickie are a place where nature proves it can be the most extraordinary artist. What delights us today with its colors is the result of geological processes and human activity in former mines. Four lakes — Purple, Blue, Green and Yellow (sometimes dried up) — lie on the slopes of Wielka Kopa (871 m) in the Rudawy Landscape Park.

Slow travel through the Colorful Lakes is a celebration of details and patience. Instead of rushing through all the lakes in one day, stop at each of them and give it attention. Walking through dense forests, you can feel how each subsequent color changes your mood and invites deeper reflection on the beauty hidden in imperfections. This is a land that rewards those who slow down — the longer you are here, the more details you notice: shades of water, changing light, rare plants.

What to see

The Colorful Lakes are a complex of four lakes, connected by a walking trail ~4 km long. Half a day is enough to see it, but it is worth devoting a full day.

The lakes

  • Purple Lake — the most famous of the colorful lakes, ~7 m deep. The water has an intense purple color, thanks to iron compounds. See it on a sunny day when the color is most intense.
  • Blue Lake — a lake with blue water, smaller than the Purple. Worth seeing — the color changes depending on light.
  • Green Lake — a lake with green water, the smallest of the three main ones. Worth seeing for the contrast of colors.
  • Yellow Lake — a lake that often dries up (especially in summer). Worth seeing for understanding the dynamics of this place.
  • Trail connecting the lakes — a walking route ~4 km long, leading through all the lakes. Easy but steep in places.

Trails and viewpoints

  • Trail to Wielka Kopa — a peak (871 m), accessible from the Colorful Lakes (~1 hour). Worth it for views over Rudawy and the Karkonosze.
  • Trail to Sokolik — a peak (642 m), with castle ruins. Worth it for lesser-known views.
  • Viewpoint above Purple Lake — a viewpoint from which you can see the lake from above. Worth it for photography.
  • Nature trail in Rudawy Landscape Park — an educational trail, leading through various habitats. Good for families with children.
  • Trail to Bolczów Castle — ruins of a 14th-century castle, ~3 km from the Colorful Lakes. Worth it for history and views.

Surroundings

  • Rudawy Janowickie — the mountain range in which the Colorful Lakes lie. Worth it for walks and contact with nature.
  • Bolczów Castle — ruins of a 14th-century castle, ~3 km from the Colorful Lakes. Worth visiting.
  • Janowice Wielkie — a village at the foot of Rudawy, with accommodation base and trailhead.
  • Karpniki — a village with a castle and lake, ~5 km from the Colorful Lakes. Worth it for architecture.
  • Jelenia Góra — a town at the foot of the Karkonosze, ~15 km from the Colorful Lakes. Worth it for accommodation base.
  • Karkonosze — the highest range of the Sudetes, ~20 km from the Colorful Lakes. Worth it for contrast.

When to go

  • May and June — the best time for slow travel. The colors of the lakes are most intense, fewer tourists, long days. Worth a walk.
  • September and October — second best. Autumn colours in the forests around the lakes, clear air, fewer people.
  • July and August — peak season. Lakes crowded, car parks full. Note: in summer the lakes may dry up (especially Yellow and Green). If you must — pick a weekday.
  • Winter — the lakes in winter are less accessible (snow, ice). Worth it for those who seek solitude.

Getting there

  • By car: ~1.5 hours from Wrocław (A4 and DK3), ~20 minutes from Jelenia Góra, ~2.5 hours from Prague. Car park in Janowice Wielkie or Karpniki (paid, in season full from 9 a.m.).
  • By train: station in Janowice Wielkie, with connections from Wrocław (~1.5 hours), Jelenia Góra (~20 minutes). Then on foot or taxi.
  • By bus: PKS and private operators from Jelenia Góra and Wrocław to Janowice Wielkie.

Public transport in the region is sparse — a car makes moving between trailheads much easier. From Janowice Wielkie to the lakes leads a walking trail (~30 minutes).

How much time you need

Half a day (2–3 hours) is the minimum to visit the Colorful Lakes and walk around Rudawy.

A full day lets you add the trail to Wielka Kopa or Bolczów Castle.

A weekend (2 days) lets you combine the Colorful Lakes with the Karkonosze or Jelenia Góra.

What to eat and where to sleep

  • Lower Silesian cuisine — duck with apples, Silesian dumplings, kwaśnica. Worth trying in local inns.
  • Grilled trout — from local farms in the Sudetes.
  • Grilled oscypek — smoked sheep cheese with cranberry, available at shepherd huts on the meadows.
  • Beer from local breweries — several craft breweries in the Sudetes.
  • Coffee and cake — Jelenia Góra and Karpniki have several good cafés.
  • Korycin cheeses — smoked and aged, available in local shops.

Accommodation: Janowice Wielkie (closest to the lakes, smaller choice), Karpniki (village with a castle, close by), Jelenia Góra (larger town, more options), Karpacz (base for the Karkonosze), Kowary (small town with accommodation).

Practical tips

  • Bookings — entry tickets to the Colorful Lakes are available on site (no need to book). However, come in the morning to avoid crowds.
  • Weather — Sudeten weather changes quickly. Even in July, it can rain. Pack a rain jacket, hat and gloves.
  • Shoes — comfortable sports shoes are essential on the trail around the lakes. The route is easy but steep in places.
  • Colors — the best colors of the lakes are on sunny days, when the sun illuminates the water. Come in the morning or at noon.
  • Water and food — there are no food or water points on the trail. Bring your own.
  • Mobile signal — good in Janowice Wielkie, weaker deep in Rudawy.
  • Drying lakes — some lakes (especially Yellow and Green) dry up in summer. Check the water level before your trip.

Trip plan

  1. Trail linking the Purple, Blue, and Green lakes
  2. Photography in shifting natural light
  3. Short hike into Rudawy Janowickie hills

Tip

Take a walk on a lightly cloudy day. Diffused light best emphasizes the depth of the lakes' colors, making them even more intense.
