LUBUSZ

Ujście Warty National Park — Bird Kingdom

1 day
Home›Lubusz›Ujście Warty National Park

Warta Mouth National Park is a paradise for birds. Warta floodplains, oxbow lakes, swans, and geese. Slow travel through the Lubusz Land.

Why it's worth it

Warta Mouth National Park is the youngest national park in Poland (established 2001) and one of the most interesting in terms of nature. It lies in the Lubusz voivodeship, at the mouth of the Warta into the Oder. It protects extensive floodplains, oxbow lakes, meadows, and reed beds — some of the most valuable wetland areas in Poland. This is a paradise for birds: about 280 species live in the park, including many rare and endangered ones, such as geese, swans, cranes, and eagles. In autumn and spring, the park becomes a place of mass bird migrations — thousands of geese and ducks pass through.

Slow travel through Warta Mouth National Park is a journey through a landscape that requires humility. The park has no peaks or waterfalls — there are floodplains, meadows, and birds. It is worth spending 1–2 days here to truly feel this landscape. The best way to explore the park is on foot or by bike — there are several nature trails, including the “Ptasi Raj” (Bird Paradise) trail. Public transport is weak, so rent a car.

What to see

Warta Mouth National Park is divided into three main parts: the Warta floodplains (centre), the Oder oxbow lakes (west), and the meadows in the northern part. Exploring the park takes 1–2 days.

Warta floodplains

  • Warta floodplains — the most valuable ecosystem in the park, a refuge for wetland birds. Worth it for nature.
  • “Ptasi Raj” trail — a nature trail with a viewing tower. Worth it for views.
  • Słońsk village — a village with the park management and a museum. Worth it for information.

Oder oxbow lakes

  • Oder oxbow lakes — old river beds with rich aquatic vegetation. Worth it for nature.
  • “Oxbow lakes” trail — a nature trail around the oxbow lakes. Worth it for nature.
  • Górzyca village — a village with a wooden church and a former manor. Worth it for architecture.

Northern meadows

  • Flood meadows — extensive meadows, flooded in spring. Worth it for birds.
  • “Meadows” trail — a nature trail through the meadows. Worth it for nature.
  • Kostrzyn nad Odrą village — a town at the mouth of the Warta into the Oder, with a fortress. Worth it for history.

When to go

  • April–May — the best time for birds. Spring migrations, birds at peak nesting.
  • May and June — the best time for slow travel. Warm, green, few tourists.
  • September–November — second best. Autumn bird migrations, thousands of geese and ducks.
  • December–March — the park is accessible in winter, but less interesting for nature. Worth it for those seeking silence.

Getting there

  • By car: ~40 minutes from Gorzów (DK22), ~2 hours from Szczecin (DK31), ~1.5 hours from Berlin (A12).
  • By train: Kostrzyn nad Odrą, Słońsk have railway stations. Then rent a car.
  • By bus: PKS serves most larger villages, but connections are sparse.

Warta Mouth National Park is best explored by car or bike — the park is vast, and public transport is weak.

How much time you need

Half a day is the minimum — the “Ptasi Raj” trail and a viewpoint.

A weekend (2 days) is realistic for a first visit. “Ptasi Raj” trail, “Oxbow lakes” trail, “Meadows” trail.

Three days lets you explore the whole park and surroundings — from Kostrzyn to Górzyca.

What to eat and where to sleep

  • Pierogi with potatoes — a Lubusz classic, available at inns.
  • Potato kiszka — a Lubusz speciality, available at inns.
  • Trout from the Warta — from local farms, available at inns.
  • Goat cheeses — from local farms, available in village shops.

Accommodation: Kostrzyn nad Odrą (town, more options), Słońsk (village, guesthouses), Górzyca (village, agritourism), numerous agritourism farms in villages.

Practical tips

  • Guided tours — the park has rich birdlife, take a guide.
  • Binoculars — essential equipment. Without binoculars you miss half the experience.
  • Shopping — buy honey, cheese, and cold cuts directly from farmers.
  • Opening hours — trails are open year-round, but sections may be closed during the breeding season.
  • Weather — weather can be changeable, even in July bring a jacket.
  • Signal — weak in many parts of the park. Have an offline map.

Trip plan

  1. Observation tower in Chyrzyno
  2. Bird Paradise Trail
  3. Cruise on the Warta

Tip

Go to the “Ptasi Raj” trail early in the morning — the best time to hear and see wetland birds.
