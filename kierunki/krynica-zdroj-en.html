Krynica-Zdrój — Pearl of Polish Spas

2 days
Home›Lesser Poland›Krynica-Zdrój

Krynica-Zdrój is the largest spa in Poland. Mineral waters, Spa Park, Beskid Sądecki. Slow travel through Małopolska.

Why it's worth it

Krynica-Zdrój is the largest spa in Poland and one of the oldest — the first mentions of healing waters date back to the 16th century. It lies in the Małopolska voivodeship, in the Beskid Sądecki, in the Kryniczanka valley. Krynica is a town that has everything a spa should have: mineral waters (over 20 springs), a Spa Park, pump rooms, spa houses, a promenade, a summer theatre, and walking alleys. Add to that unique cultural heritage: the Nikifor Museum (naive painter), Lemko Orthodox churches, and numerous monuments in the area.

Slow travel through Krynica-Zdrój is a journey through a landscape that surprises with its diversity. The town has a spa, parks, a promenade, and mountains — all within a few kilometres. It is worth spending 3–4 days here to truly feel this landscape. The best way to explore Krynica is on foot, and the Beskid Sądecki by car or on foot — public transport from Nowy Sącz and Kraków is good (trains, buses).

What to see

Krynica-Zdrój is divided into three main parts: the spa centre (Spa Park), Jaworzyna Krynicka (mountain), and the surroundings (Lemko Orthodox churches). Exploring the town and surroundings takes 3–4 days.

Spa centre

  • Spa Park — a park with mineral water pump rooms, alleys, and a concert shell. Worth it for walks.
  • Krynica Promenade — the main promenade of the town, with numerous cafés and shops. Worth it for walks.
  • Main Pump Room — a place where you can taste local waters. Worth it for health.

Jaworzyna Krynicka

  • Jaworzyna Krynicka — a peak above Krynica (1,114 m), with a gondola lift. Worth it for views.
  • Gondola lift — a lift to Jaworzyna, operating year-round. Worth it for views.
  • Trail to Jaworzyna — a hiking trail to the summit, about 2 hours. Worth it for hiking.

Surroundings

  • Powroźnik Orthodox church — a 17th-century wooden Lemko Orthodox church, UNESCO-listed. Worth it for architecture.
  • Tylicz Orthodox church — a wooden Lemko Orthodox church, one of the most valuable in the region. Worth it for architecture.
  • Muszyna — a small town with a castle and sensory gardens. Worth it for architecture.

When to go

  • May and June — the best time for slow travel. Warm, green, few tourists.
  • July–August — spa and trail season. Worth it for health, but crowded in the Spa Park.
  • September–October — second best. Autumn colours, fewer people.
  • November–March — the town is sleepy in winter, but the spa operates. Worth it for treatments and silence.

Getting there

  • By car: ~2 hours from Kraków (DK75), ~2 hours from Rzeszów (DK28), ~5 hours from Warsaw (S7 + DK75).
  • By train: Krynica-Zdrój has a railway station, with connections from Kraków and Nowy Sącz.
  • By bus: PKS serves Nowy Sącz, Krynica, Muszyna.

Krynica is best explored on foot — the town is small, and attractions are close together. The Beskid Sądecki is best explored on foot or by car.

How much time you need

A weekend (2 days) is the minimum — Spa Park, Promenade, Jaworzyna Krynicka.

Three days is realistic for a first visit. Spa Park, Promenade, Jaworzyna Krynicka, Powroźnik Orthodox church.

A week lets you explore all of Krynica and the Beskid Sądecki — from Muszyna to Tylicz.

What to eat and where to sleep

  • Kwaśnica — sour cabbage soup, a highlander classic.
  • Pierogi with bryndza — regional pierogi with sheep cheese, available at inns.
  • Trout from the Kryniczanka — from local farms, available at inns.
  • Mineral waters — from local springs, available at pump rooms.
  • Beer from Małopolska breweries — several craft breweries in the region.

Accommodation: Krynica-Zdrój (town, more options), Muszyna (small town, guesthouses), Nowy Sącz (town, more options), Tylicz (village, agritourism).

Practical tips

  • Guided tours — Krynica's history is rich, take a guide.
  • Mineral waters — Krynica has over 20 springs. Taste several different ones.
  • Shopping — buy honey, cheese, and cold cuts directly from farmers.
  • Jaworzyna Krynicka — the gondola lift operates year-round. Buy tickets online.
  • Weather — Beskid Sądecki weather can be changeable, even in July bring a jacket.
  • Signal — good throughout the region, including in the mountains.

Trip plan

  1. Promenade and pump room walk
  2. Funicular to Parkowa Hill
  3. Evening at the Modrzejewska Theatre

Tip

Take the gondola lift to Jaworzyna Krynicka — on a clear day you can see the Tatras, Pieniny, and Beskid Sądecki.
