LOWER SILESIA

Kudowa-Zdrój — Breathe in the Mountains

2 days
Home›Lower Silesia›Kudowa-Zdrój

Kudowa-Zdrój is the oldest spa in the Sudetes. Mineral waters, Spa Park, Table Mountains. Slow travel through the Kłodzko Land.

Why it's worth it

Kudowa-Zdrój is the oldest spa in the Sudetes and one of the oldest in Poland — the first mentions of healing waters date back to the 15th century. It lies in the Lower Silesian voivodeship, in the Kłodzko Valley, at the foot of the Table Mountains. Kudowa is a town that has everything a spa should have: mineral waters, a Spa Park, a pump room, a spa house, a summer theatre, and walking alleys. Add to that unique cultural heritage: the Skull Chapel in Czermna (one of the few in Europe), the Toy Museum, and numerous monuments in the area.

Slow travel through Kudowa-Zdrój is a journey through a landscape that surprises with its diversity. The town has a spa, parks, a skull chapel, and mountains — all within a few kilometres. It is worth spending 2–3 days here to truly feel this landscape. The best way to explore Kudowa is on foot, and the Table Mountains by car or on foot — public transport from Kłodzko and Wrocław is good (trains, buses).

What to see

Kudowa-Zdrój is divided into three main parts: the spa centre (Spa Park), Czermna (skull chapel), and the Table Mountains (Szczeliniec Wielki). Exploring the town and surroundings takes 2–3 days.

Spa centre

  • Spa Park — a park with a mineral water pump room, alleys, and a concert shell. Worth it for walks.
  • Mineral Water Pump Room — a place where you can taste local waters. Worth it for health.
  • Summer Theatre — a wooden theatre in the Spa Park, with concerts and performances. Worth it for culture.

Czermna

  • Skull Chapel — a unique chapel whose walls and ceiling are covered with human bones. Worth it for history.
  • St Bartholomew's Church — a 17th-century wooden church, one of the most valuable in the region. Worth it for architecture.
  • Czermna village — a village with the skull chapel and a wooden church. Worth it for culture.

Table Mountains

  • Szczeliniec Wielki — the highest peak in the Table Mountains (919 m), with a rock labyrinth. Worth it for views.
  • Błędne Skały — a rock labyrinth in the Table Mountains, unique in Poland. Worth it for views.
  • Karłów — a hamlet at the foot of Szczeliniec, starting point for trails. Worth it as a base.

When to go

  • May and June — the best time for slow travel. Warm, green, few tourists.
  • July–August — spa and trail season. Worth it for health, but crowded in the Spa Park.
  • September–October — second best. Autumn colours, fewer people.
  • November–March — the town is sleepy in winter, but the spa operates. Worth it for treatments and silence.

Getting there

  • By car: ~1.5 hours from Wrocław (DK8), ~2 hours from Prague (D11), ~4 hours from Berlin (A4 + DK8).
  • By train: Kudowa-Zdrój has a railway station, with connections from Wrocław and Kłodzko.
  • By bus: PKS serves Kłodzko, Kudowa, Karłów.

Kudowa is best explored on foot — the town is small, and attractions are close together. The Table Mountains are best explored on foot or by car.

How much time you need

A weekend (2 days) is the minimum — Spa Park, Skull Chapel, Szczeliniec Wielki.

Three days is realistic for a first visit. Spa Park, Skull Chapel, Szczeliniec Wielki, Błędne Skały.

A week lets you explore all of Kudowa and the Table Mountains — from Karłów to Polanica.

What to eat and where to sleep

  • Pierogi with meat — a Lower Silesian classic, available at inns.
  • Duck with apples — from local farms, available at inns.
  • Trout from the Bystrzyca — from local farms, available at inns.
  • Mineral waters — from local springs, available at the pump room.
  • Beer from Lower Silesian breweries — several craft breweries in the region.

Accommodation: Kudowa-Zdrój (town, more options), Kłodzko (town, more options), Polanica-Zdrój (spa, guesthouses), Karłów (village, guesthouses).

Practical tips

  • Guided tours — Kudowa's history is rich, take a guide.
  • Skull Chapel — daily entry limits apply. Book tickets in advance.
  • Shopping — buy honey, cheese, and cold cuts directly from farmers.
  • Szczeliniec Wielki — entry tickets required in season. Buy online in advance.
  • Weather — Sudeten weather can be changeable, even in July bring a jacket.
  • Signal — good throughout the region, including in the mountains.

Trip plan

  1. Walk through the central Spa Park
  2. Mountain ridge viewpoints
  3. Dinner in a cozy local guesthouse

Tip

Visit the Skull Chapel in Czermna — one of the most unusual places in Poland and one of the few of its kind in Europe.
