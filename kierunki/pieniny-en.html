Pieniny — Dunajec River Perspective

2 days
Home›Lesser Poland›Pieniny

Pieniny is one of the most beautiful mountain ranges in Poland. Three Crowns, Dunajec Gorge, castles. Slow travel on the Polish-Slovak border.

Why it's worth it

Pieniny is one of the most beautiful mountain ranges in Poland and unique in Europe. It lies on the border with Slovakia, in the Małopolska voivodeship. The highest peak is Wysokie Skałki (1,050 m), but the symbol of Pieniny is Three Crowns (982 m) — a distinctive peak with three summits. Pieniny has a unique landscape: limestone rocks, the Dunajec Gorge (one of the most beautiful in Europe), meadows, forests, and castles. These mountains are lower than the Tatras but more picturesque and less crowded.

Slow travel through Pieniny is a journey through a landscape that surprises with its diversity. The mountains have rocky peaks, river gorges, castles, and villages — all within a few kilometres of each other. It is worth spending 2–3 days here to truly feel this landscape. The best way to explore Pieniny is on foot or by kayak — there are about 100 km of hiking trails and kayak routes. Public transport from Nowy Targ and Szczawnica is good (buses, minibuses).

What to see

Pieniny is divided into three main parts: the Proper Pieniny (Three Crowns), the Czorsztyn Pieniny (castle), and the Little Pieniny (Wysokie Skałki). Exploring the mountains takes 2–3 days.

Proper Pieniny

  • Three Crowns — a distinctive peak (982 m), with a viewpoint. Worth it for views.
  • Dunajec Gorge — one of the most beautiful gorges in Europe. Worth it for a raft trip.
  • Sokolica — a peak above the Dunajec Gorge (747 m). Worth it for views.

Czorsztyn Pieniny

  • Czorsztyn Castle — ruins of a castle above Lake Czorsztyn. Worth it for architecture.
  • Niedzica Castle — a castle above Lake Czorsztyn, one of the best-preserved in Poland. Worth it for architecture.
  • Lake Czorsztyn — a reservoir on the Dunajec, with numerous harbours. Worth it for sailing.

Little Pieniny

  • Wysokie Skałki — the highest peak in Pieniny (1,050 m). Worth it for views.
  • Jaworki village — a village at the foot of the Little Pieniny, with an Orthodox church. Worth it for architecture.
  • Homole Gorge — a gorge in the Little Pieniny, one of the most beautiful in Poland. Worth it for views.

When to go

  • May and June — the best time for slow travel. Warm, green, few tourists.
  • July–August — Dunajec rafting season. Worth it for rafting, but crowded in Szczawnica and Czorsztyn.
  • September–October — second best. Autumn colours, fewer people.
  • November–April — Pieniny in winter is accessible to experienced tourists. Worth it for skiers.

Getting there

  • By car: ~2 hours from Kraków (DK47), ~1 hour from Zakopane (DK47), ~5.5 hours from Warsaw (S7 + DK47).
  • By train: Nowy Targ, Szczawnica have railway stations. Then rent a car.
  • By bus: PKS and private carriers from Kraków, Nowy Targ, Zakopane.

Pieniny is best explored on foot or by kayak — the mountains are well marked, and public transport from Nowy Targ and Szczawnica is good.

How much time you need

A weekend (2 days) is the minimum — Three Crowns, Dunajec rafting.

Three days is realistic for a first visit. Three Crowns, Dunajec rafting, Sokolica, Czorsztyn Castle, Niedzica Castle.

A week lets you explore all of Pieniny — from Three Crowns to Wysokie Skałki, with numerous stops.

What to eat and where to sleep

  • Kwaśnica — sour cabbage soup, a highlander classic.
  • Oscypek — smoked sheep cheese, available at shepherd huts.
  • Trout from the Dunajec — from local farms, available at inns.
  • Beer from Podhale breweries — several craft breweries in the region.

Accommodation: Szczawnica (small town, more options), Krościenko nad Dunajcem (small town, guesthouses), Czorsztyn (village, guesthouses), Jaworki (village, agritourism).

Practical tips

  • Guided tours — Pieniny has rich history and nature, take a guide.
  • Dunajec rafting — raft trips run from April to October. Book tickets in advance.
  • Shopping — buy oscypek, bryndza, and honey directly from highlanders.
  • Tickets — park entry tickets are mandatory. Buy online in advance.
  • Weather — Pieniny weather can be changeable, even in July bring a jacket.
  • Signal — good throughout the region, weaker deep in the mountains.

Trip plan

  1. Trzy Korony peak at sunrise
  2. Dunajec River rafting
  3. Walk through Szczawnica spa town

Tip

Take a raft through the Dunajec Gorge early in the morning — the best time to see the gorge without crowds and with good light for photos.
