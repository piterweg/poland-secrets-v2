Szczawnica — Spa at the Foot of the Pieniny

2 days
Home›Lesser Poland›Szczawnica

Szczawnica is a spa in the Pieniny, on the Dunajec. Mineral waters, Spa Park, Dunajec rafting. Slow travel through Małopolska.

Why it's worth it

Szczawnica is a spa in the Pieniny, on the Dunajec, in the Małopolska voivodeship. It is one of the oldest spas in Poland — the first mentions of healing waters date back to the 16th century. Szczawnica is a town that has everything a spa should have: mineral waters (over 10 springs), a Spa Park, pump rooms, spa houses, a promenade, and walking alleys. Add to that a unique location: Szczawnica lies at the foot of the Pieniny, on the Dunajec, from where you can start a raft trip through the Dunajec Gorge — one of the most beautiful in Europe.

Slow travel through Szczawnica is a journey through a landscape that surprises with its diversity. The town has a spa, parks, a promenade, a river, and mountains — all within a few kilometres. It is worth spending 2–3 days here to truly feel this landscape. The best way to explore Szczawnica is on foot, and the Pieniny by car or on foot — public transport from Nowy Targ and Kraków is good (buses, minibuses).

What to see

Szczawnica is divided into three main parts: the spa centre (Spa Park), the Dunajec Gorge (rafting), and the Pieniny (Three Crowns). Exploring the town and surroundings takes 2–3 days.

Spa centre

  • Spa Park — a park with mineral water pump rooms, alleys, and a concert shell. Worth it for walks.
  • Szczawnica Promenade — the main promenade of the town, with numerous cafés and shops. Worth it for walks.
  • Water Pump Room — a place where you can taste local waters. Worth it for health.

Dunajec Gorge

  • Dunajec rafting — raft trips through the Dunajec Gorge, about 2.5 hours. Worth it for views.
  • Dunajec Gorge — one of the most beautiful gorges in Europe. Worth it for views.
  • Raft harbour — the starting point for raft trips, in Szczawnica. Worth it for organisation.

Pieniny

  • Three Crowns — a distinctive peak (982 m), with a viewpoint. Worth it for views.
  • Sokolica — a peak above the Dunajec Gorge (747 m). Worth it for views.
  • Czorsztyn Castle — ruins of a castle above Lake Czorsztyn. Worth it for architecture.

When to go

  • May and June — the best time for slow travel. Warm, green, few tourists.
  • July–August — Dunajec rafting season. Worth it for rafting, but crowded in Szczawnica.
  • September–October — second best. Autumn colours, fewer people.
  • November–March — the town is sleepy in winter, but the spa operates. Worth it for treatments and silence.

Getting there

  • By car: ~2 hours from Kraków (DK47), ~1 hour from Zakopane (DK47), ~5.5 hours from Warsaw (S7 + DK47).
  • By train: Nowy Targ has a railway station. Then rent a car or take a minibus.
  • By bus: PKS and private carriers from Kraków, Nowy Targ, Zakopane.

Szczawnica is best explored on foot — the town is small, and attractions are close together. The Pieniny is best explored on foot or by car.

How much time you need

A weekend (2 days) is the minimum — Spa Park, Promenade, Dunajec rafting.

Three days is realistic for a first visit. Spa Park, Promenade, Dunajec rafting, Three Crowns, Czorsztyn Castle.

A week lets you explore all of Szczawnica and the Pieniny — from Krościenko to Czorsztyn.

What to eat and where to sleep

  • Kwaśnica — sour cabbage soup, a highlander classic.
  • Oscypek — smoked sheep cheese, available at shepherd huts.
  • Trout from the Dunajec — from local farms, available at inns.
  • Mineral waters — from local springs, available at pump rooms.
  • Beer from Podhale breweries — several craft breweries in the region.

Accommodation: Szczawnica (town, more options), Krościenko nad Dunajcem (small town, guesthouses), Czorsztyn (village, guesthouses), Jaworki (village, agritourism).

Practical tips

  • Guided tours — Szczawnica's history is rich, take a guide.
  • Dunajec rafting — raft trips run from April to October. Book tickets in advance.
  • Shopping — buy oscypek, bryndza, and honey directly from highlanders.
  • Three Crowns — entry tickets required in season. Buy online in advance.
  • Weather — Pieniny weather can be changeable, even in July bring a jacket.
  • Signal — good throughout the region, weaker deep in the mountains.

Trip plan

  1. Walk through the Lower and Upper Park
  2. Mineral water pump room
  3. View of the Pieniny from Palenica

Tip

Take a raft through the Dunajec Gorge early in the morning — the best time to see the gorge without crowds and with good light for photos.
