Poland on Two Wheels

Poland has over 20,000 km of marked cycling routes, a few of which are of European class. Routes of the Velo type are not just roads — they are a complete tourism product: signage, infrastructure, accommodation, bike workshops.

Slow travel by bike is a completely different experience from touring by car. A pace of 15–20 km/h lets you notice things that escape from a car window: roadside shrines, the scent of meadows, conversations with locals.

Where to Start?

For a first multi-day trip — Velo Dunajec (240 km, 3–4 days). The route runs through the Pieniny, Beskid Sądecki, and Podhale, along one of the most beautiful rivers in Poland.

For the ambitious — Green Velo (over 2,000 km through 5 voivodeships). It can be ridden in full in 10–14 days, or divided into stages of 200–300 km.

For sea lovers — Velo Baltica, the coastal route through Świnoujście, Kołobrzeg, Darłowo, Łeba to Hel. Perfect for a family holiday.

How to Plan?

Don't plan too rigidly. Assume 50–60 km per day (with luggage), not 100. Book accommodation in advance, but leave a buffer day in case of fatigue or rain.

Bikes can be rented in most starting cities. Many routes have official services with a list of cyclist-friendly accommodation, workshops, and repair stations.

ALL ROUTES

Which one will you choose?

Green Velo

EASTERN POLAND • 10–14 days

Poland's Longest Cycling Route

Over 2,000 km through five voivodeships. Warmia, Masuria, Podlasie, the Lublin region, and Subcarpathia.

Velo Dunajec

LESSER POLAND • 3–4 days

By Bike Along the Dunajec

240 km through the Pieniny, Beskid Sądecki, and Podhale. A route for those who love water and mountains.

Velo Baltica

POMERANIA • 5–7 days

Baltic Coastal Cycling Route

Along the Baltic coast, through seaside towns and Słowiński National Park.

Greater Poland Loop

GREATER POLAND • 4–5 days

The Great Greater Poland Loop

600 km through Wielkopolski National Park, the Warta Valley, and the surroundings of Poznań. A route for patient cyclists.

Bug Valley

PODLASIE • 3–4 days

By Bike Through the Wild River

A route along Poland's wildest river. Villages, Orthodox churches, and Bug-side landscapes.

Augustów Canal

PODLASIE • 2–3 days

By Bike Along a 19th-Century Canal

Locks, the Augustów Primeval Forest, and lakes. A cycling route with the soul of old engineering.
