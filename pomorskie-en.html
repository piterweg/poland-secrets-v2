POMERANIA

Baltic, amber, and Hanseatic cities.

Dunes, piers, Gothic churches, and wind from the sea. Pomerania is a region where slow travel tastes like fresh fish straight from the cutter.

A Region of Two Elements

Pomerania is a land where the sea and the land conduct an endless dialogue. Wide beaches, shifting dunes, pine forests, and port cities — all within a hundred kilometers.

In spring and autumn the coast empties and becomes the perfect place for long walks. It is the time when you can hear your own footsteps and the sound of waves without the noise of holiday resorts.

From Gdańsk to Łeba

Gdańsk delights with Hanseatic architecture and shipyard heritage. Sopot tempts with Europe's longest wooden pier. Łeba and Słowiński National Park offer a Polish desert by the sea.

On Hel, a narrow spit separates the Baltic from the Bay of Puck. It is a place where you can stand at the end of Poland and look at the open sea on one side and the bay on the other.
